📞 Modern IKEv2 Protocol Engine (RFC 7296)
SECURE
Role: Manages your VPN connection lifecycle with high speed, instant auto-reconnect, and seamless network mobility across Wi-Fi and 5G.
IKEv2 acts as the intelligent digital negotiator for your VPN. Like traveling on a train where your laptop switches between station Wi-Fi and 5G cellular, MOBIKE (RFC 4555) technology shifts your encrypted session without dropping active video calls or web apps.
🔒 Authenticated AES-256-GCM Cipher (Galois/Counter Mode)
SECURE
Role: Bank-grade 256-bit encryption that scrambles data while simultaneously attaching a 128-bit tamper-proof digital seal.
Think of AES-256-GCM as placing your secret documents into an unbreakable steel vault while applying a tamper-evident holographic seal to the outside. If a hacker alters even 1 bit of data, the server detects the broken seal and discards the packet instantly before decryption.
🤝 Elliptic Curve Secret Handshake (DH Group 19 / NIST ECP-256)
SECURE
Role: Allows two remote servers across the open internet to safely agree on identical secret encryption keys without ever sending the key over the wire.
Imagine two people in a room full of eavesdroppers mixing base colors publicly to end up with the exact same secret color mixture. Group 19 uses NIST P-256 Elliptic Curve math to compute shared secrets 10x faster than legacy 2048-bit prime numbers, dramatically saving battery power.
🔑 Ephemeral One-Time Rekeying (PFS Enforced)
SECURE
Role: Constantly generates brand-new, independent session keys so compromising today's key leaves all past and future recorded traffic 100% safe.
Imagine a hotel keycard system where every single room keycard is completely unique and automatically expires after 1 hour, rather than having one master key. With PFS enforced (CREATE_CHILD_SA), even if a hacker steals the server's master key in the future, past recorded traffic remains un-decryptable.
🛡️ IPsec Tunnel Mode Encapsulation (Complete Outer IP Envelope)
SECURE
Role: Encloses your entire original IP packet—including private source and destination IP addresses—inside a brand-new encrypted outer IP envelope.
Like placing a coded postcard inside a thick, sealed courier envelope addressed between two secure VPN gateways. Eavesdroppers on public networks cannot inspect internal company IP addresses, device names, or private network topology.
