Plain-English Component Rationale (For Non-Experts)
Modern Tunnel Negotiator (IKEv2) SECURE

Plain-English Role: Establishes and manages the secure VPN connection with speed, auto-reconnect, and multi-network stability.

IKEv2 handles connection negotiation and roaming seamlessly (switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data without dropping your tunnel).

Bank-Grade Vault Lock (AES-256-GCM) SECURE

Plain-English Role: High-speed encryption that scrambles your data so hackers cannot read it, with built-in tamper detection.

AEAD mode ensures that any altered or forged packets are rejected immediately before decryption.

Elliptic Curve Secret Handshake (DH Group 19) SECURE

Plain-English Role: Allows two servers to agree on secret encryption keys across the open internet without anyone overhearing.

Elliptic Curve math (ECP-256) provides 256-bit cryptographic strength with minimal CPU overhead.

Temporary One-Time Keys (PFS Enforced) SECURE

Plain-English Role: Generates fresh session keys constantly so compromising today's key leaves past and future traffic completely safe.

Even if a long-term master key is compromised in the future, past recorded sessions cannot be retroactively decrypted.
