Automating Human Bottlenecks in IPsec Operations

Traditional VPN security audits happen annually or quarterly through labor-intensive sampling in Wireshark. In modern dynamic multi-cloud environments, tunnel configurations drift silently:

  • Configuration Drift: Temporary fallbacks to permissive ciphers (e.g. 3DES or DH Group 2) for emergency interoperability often persist indefinitely unnoticed.
  • Rekey Expiration: Missing or miscalculated SA lifetime limits trigger silent dropped sessions or perpetual key reuse.
  • Continuous Assurance: Sentinel transitions audit assurance from point-in-time snapshots to an automated, continuous verification pipeline.
